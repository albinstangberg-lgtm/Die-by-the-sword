Die by the Sword

Move the mouse to move your sword arm. It is a physical limb, not an animation: it has weight, it lags, and it will bury itself in the walls.

W and S walk. A and D turn — the mouse is your arm, not the camera. Q and E sidestep, Space jumps.

Hold the right button and move left or right to roll the cutting edge. Hold the left button to move your other arm instead — and the shield on it, once you have one.

X puts your sword on your back and takes it out again; a sheathed sword leaves a hand free, and F picks things up with it. H drinks a potion, C crouches, and Space running at something waist high vaults it. A shield hangs on the rack by the wall.

An orc with an axe and a goblin with a spear are across the room. Neither will tell you what it is about to do. Watch the weapon: every swing starts with it going back, and the heavier it is the longer that takes. Read it, then be somewhere else.
